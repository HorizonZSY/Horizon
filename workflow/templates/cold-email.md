# 套磁邮件模板

结构参考 mak-raiaan/ColdEmailPhDMSc 与 zhanglj37/Tutorial-on-PhD-Application，按英国/欧洲经济学申博场景调整。总长度 200–280 词。

## 模板

```
Subject: Prospective PhD applicant (<entry year>) – <topic in 4–8 words>

Dear Professor <Surname>,

[P1 — who I am, 40–50 words]
I am completing an MSc in <programme> at <university>. My dissertation
examines <question> using <method> and <data>.

[P2 — why you, ≤120 words — WRITTEN BY THE APPLICANT]
<Reference one specific paper of theirs and one specific element of it
(data, design, finding). State the question it raises for you and how it
connects to your proposed project.>

[P3 — what I propose, 60–80 words]
I plan to study <one-sentence question>. The design uses <identification
strategy> with <data>, and would <expected contribution>. A two-page summary
is attached.

[P4 — the ask, 1–2 sentences]
Would you be taking new PhD students for <entry year>, and could this project
fall within your supervision? If so, I would also be grateful to know whether
you would support an application to <funding route, e.g., a DTP studentship>.

Kind regards,
<Full name>
<Current programme, university>
<Link to CV / personal page>
```

## 规则

- 第 2 段由申请者本人写。可以请 `/phd-outreach` 提供可核实的连接点并批注初稿，但不要让 AI 直接生成这一段；泛化的恭维很容易被识别
- 第 2 段自检：把导师名字换成别人，这段话还成立吗？成立就说明不够具体
- 附件不超过 2 个 PDF：CV、proposal 摘要；文件名 `Surname_CV.pdf`、`Surname_Proposal_Summary.pdf`
- 不提 GPA，除非它是明显优势；不写"I am very passionate about"之类的空话
- 使用毕业后仍长期可用的邮箱
- 同一院系一次只联系一位导师

## 跟进邮件（发出 10 个工作日后无回复）

```
Subject: Re: Prospective PhD applicant (<entry year>) – <topic>

Dear Professor <Surname>,

I am following up on my email below about a PhD project on <topic>. I would
be grateful to know whether you are considering new students for <entry year>.

Kind regards,
<Name>
```

跟进后再过 10 个工作日仍无回复：记为 `no_reply`，可以联系同院系的下一位导师。
